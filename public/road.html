Road page

Jot down trip costs, receipt photos, travel time and checks received on the road, even with no signal. They wait in the "From the road" box in zAccountant until you accept them at your desk.

At your desk: Trips → Your phone on the road → Make a road key, then scan the code with this phone's camera.
