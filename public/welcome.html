Services

Books

Double-entry books checked by the database itself: every entry balances, closed months stay closed, and a tamper-evident audit trail records every change. Balance sheet, profit and loss, cash flow and trial balance, as PDF or Excel.

Invoices and bills

Invoices with your logo and letterhead, hourly rates and costs passed on, sales tax, customer payments and aging. Bills and vendor payments, with 1099 vendors flagged.

Banking

Import your bank's CSV or QFX statements, match them to your books, set rules for repeat items, and reconcile each month.

Payroll add-on

Paychecks with federal and state withholding for Texas, Colorado, California and Washington DC, pay stubs, and the payroll taxes owed with their due dates.

Timesheets add-on

Time by job, approvals by department, an import from the HoursTracker app, and approved hours straight into payroll with overtime worked out.

Tax-ready add-on

Your books summarized on the lines of your federal return (Schedule C, 1065, 1120-S or 1120), 1099-NEC totals per vendor, and W-2 / W-3 totals, ready for your tax preparer.

Bring your books

Import your chart of accounts, opening balances and transactions from QuickBooks or Excel. Every import is checked first and can be undone.

Security

Today

  • All traffic between you and zAccountant is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Social Security numbers and two-step sign-in secrets are encrypted before they are stored.
  • Passwords are never stored, only a one-way scrambled form of them.
  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app is required for every account.
  • Each company's books are walled off by the database itself.

Coming planned

  • Full encryption on your side: your books encrypted on your own device before they are sent, so we store only encrypted information and even we can't read it.
  • Data stored on servers in Switzerland.

These are not in place yet during alpha testing.
