# zAccountant User Guide

zAccountant keeps a small business's books, sends invoices, pays bills, reconciles the bank and runs payroll, for
companies in Texas, Colorado, California and Washington DC.

This guide is arranged **by tier**: what everyone uses first, then what each plan adds. Read the chapters for the
tiers your company has. Inside the app, the **? Help** button opens the short guide for the screen you are on, and
**🐞 Beta feedback** sends us a problem report.

| Tier | Chapter | Who has it |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | [Everyone: signing in, your company, finding your way, when something goes wrong](tier-0-everyone.md) | every person |
| 1 | [Books (every plan): transactions, invoices, bills, banking, reports, settings](tier-1-books.md) | every company |
| 2 | [Payroll: employees, paychecks, payroll taxes](tier-2-payroll.md) | the Company plan |
| 3 | [Timesheets add-on: time entry, approval, HoursTracker](tier-3-timesheets.md) | companies with Timesheets |
| 4 | [zAccountant staff: the Admin and Developer dashboards](tier-4-staff.md) | zAccountant's own team |

**During the beta, every company is on the Beta tester plan, which includes every tier for free.** After launch, a
company subscribes, monthly or yearly at a reduced price, to *Family and individuals* or *Company* (which includes
payroll), and adds Tax-ready or Timesheets as needed. Prices are on the Prices page (`/prices`).

## What your role lets you do

Your company's owner gives each person a role. If a button you expect is missing, your role or your company's plan
doesn't include it; ask the owner.

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | everything, including settings, people, and closing or reopening the books |
| Accountant | the books, reports, imports and payroll (not company settings) |
| Bookkeeper | day-to-day transactions, banking, invoices and bills, own time |
| Viewer | read the books and reports; changes nothing |
| Staff | write invoices and enter their own time |
| Custom roles | whatever mix of permissions the owner ticks |

## Words used in this guide

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Account | a "bucket" money is sorted into: a bank account, equipment, a loan, an income or expense category |
| Chart of accounts | the list of all your accounts |
| Entry (journal entry) | one transaction, recorded as money leaving one or more accounts and arriving in others. It always balances |
| Debit / credit | the two sides of an entry. You rarely need them; the simple screens handle them |
| Accounts Receivable (AR) | what customers owe you (unpaid invoices) |
| Accounts Payable (AP) | what you owe vendors (unpaid bills) |
| Reconcile | tick off your records against the bank statement until they agree to the penny |
| Close the books | lock a period so nothing dated in it can change |
| Void | cancel a saved invoice, bill, payment or paycheck. Its entry is reversed and it stays on record, marked void |
| Edit (of an invoice, bill, payment or trip cost) | the old record is voided and a corrected copy takes its place in one step, so both stay on record |
| Project | a job you bill for: number, name, customer and site address. Time, passed-on costs and trips are tied to projects |
| Trip | a journey with its costs (airfare, hotel, mileage…) and travel time, shared among the projects it was for |
| Road page | the page on your phone (`/road`) for jotting down trip costs, receipts, travel time and checks received, even with no signal |
| Passkey | sign-in with Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or the device PIN instead of typing a password and code |
| Payment plan | an invoice paid in equal monthly parts; only a part past its own date is late |
| Late charge | monthly interest and a late-statement fee on a past-due invoice, posted as a small invoice of its own |
| Book file (`.zacc`) | one download holding everything in your company's books, for your own backup |
